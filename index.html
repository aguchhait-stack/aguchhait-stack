Featured Projects

UK Road Collision Analysis

GitHub | PDF Report

A comprehensive statistical analysis of over 50,000 road collision records to identify key environmental factors influencing accident severity.

  • Predictive Modeling of Collision Severity: Developed a Random Forest classifier achieving 74% accuracy. The model identifies relationships between Hour, Day of Week, and Speed to distinguish high-volume “Slight” incidents from high-severity crashes.
  • Hypothesis Testing & Statistical Validation: Validated situational factors by rejecting the Null Hypothesis (H₀) with p<0.05. Confirmed that variables like Speed are statistically significant predictors of Collision Severity.
  • Temporal & Distribution Analysis: Identified a Bi-modal distribution with a distinct Left Skew. Analysis shows a morning peak at 08:00 and a primary concentration between 15:00 and 17:00.
  • Volume vs. Severity Insights: Found that 30 mph zones drive Collision Severity through sheer volume, while 60 mph zones drive severity through impact force, despite fewer total incidents.

Breast Cancer Trend Analysis (Australia)

GitHub | PDF Report

A Functional Data Analysis (FDA) of 81 years of age-specific mortality rates (1921–2001) to identify long-term biological trends and medical progress.

  • Functional Data Transformation: Converted 81 annual discrete mortality datasets into continuous functional objects using a 4th-order (Cubic) B-spline basis.
  • Hierarchical GCV Optimization: Executed a three-stage “Goldilocks” search (Broad → Reduced → Zoomed) to identify the optimal smoothing parameter at \(\log_{10}(\lambda) = 1\).
  • Noise Reduction & Signal Extraction: Utilized a harmonic acceleration penalty to filter out random annual “noise,” revealing underlying shifts in mortality across age groups.

New York City Taxi Fare Prediction

GitHub | PDF Report

Performed exploratory data analysis and preprocessing on the Kaggle NYC Taxi Fare Prediction dataset to investigate pricing patterns and prepare trip-level data for predictive modeling.

  • Large-Scale Data Sampling & Preparation: Processed a 1M-row representative sample from a 55M-row transportation dataset to enable local-memory analysis while preserving large-scale distribution patterns.
  • Data Cleaning & Outlier Detection: Identified and removed anomalous records including negative fares, invalid passenger counts, and missing coordinate values to improve downstream model reliability.
  • Temporal Trend Analysis: Discovered fare increases over time and identified hourly fare variation patterns, including elevated early-morning pricing and afternoon demand peaks.
  • Feature Engineering: Derived temporal predictors from pickup timestamps including year, month, and hour to support future regression modeling.
